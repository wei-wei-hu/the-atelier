The Atelier

Overview

An AI-assisted planner for work and life. Plan what matters, make progress on your goals, and keep track of what you accomplish along the way.

See example ↓

Why I built The Atelier

  • Some days, you get so busy that the things you wanted to do never make it onto your list.Then the week ends, and you’re trying to remember what you actually got done.
  • I built The Atelier to help me decide what I want to achieve, plan my days, and keep track of my progress.AI helps me work out the next steps and write up what I’ve accomplished, so I can see what’s done and what to focus on next.

How The Atelier works

  • Set Your GoalsDecide what you want to move forward at work and in your personal life. Choose your priorities for the week and make room for the skills you want to develop.
  • Plan Your DayStart with your three most important tasks. Use short check-ins to adjust your plans, record what you completed, and keep track of what still needs attention.
  • Track Your AccomplishmentsLet AI turn your daily notes into weekly accomplishment summaries. Each month, review your strongest examples, identify who should hear about them, and prepare for your next career conversation.

See an example week

  • Set Your GoalsFinish an online AI course, run or walk four times, and plan meals and money for the month.
  • Plan Your DayWednesday’s focus: Build a healthy routine. A 30-minute run, meal prep, and trying AI to draft the weekly report.
  • Review your day3 of 4 done. Still open: read 20 pages, by Thursday night.
  • Track Your Accomplishments“Used AI to draft my weekly report and cut it from an hour to 20 minutes.” On Friday, the week becomes a one-pager.
